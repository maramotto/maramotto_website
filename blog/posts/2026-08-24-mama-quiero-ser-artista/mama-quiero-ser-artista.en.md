**Yes, I want to be an artist.** And no, I don't want to do anything else
with my time and my energy. I want to devote myself to art 24/7.

Because as *Mamá quiero ser artista* — the great Concha Velasco's song — puts it:

> "And she thought, and rightly so,
 That there are only two kinds of people, The artists, and everyone else"

**And I'm deciding this at 37.**

I have no knowledge of the art world. No studies, no contacts, nothing
connecting me to this industry, this market. I don't know exactly what I
want to do or how I'm going to make it happen.

Sounds like I've got it all figured out, right? I have no idea. I don't
know where it's going to take me, or whether I'll ever get anywhere
interesting. I don't know.

All I know is that I don't want to do anything else.

I'm not an idealist, and I haven't lost my mind. I know starting
something is always hard, that it has its frustrating moments, and that
things will move, for sure, much more slowly than I'd like. I'm not
going to do anything crazy — I'm not quitting my job (for now), and I
have no deadlines or goals. I just know I want to devote myself to art,
because it's what makes me feel alive. What helps me carry the weight of
existing. What, for small moments and instants, makes being alive worth
all the pain.

I have no idea what will happen. I just know I want to try. I owe it to
myself. It's about time I became who I've always wanted to be. Because
what I have always wanted to be is an artist.
